# Watch this car — implementation and verification

Date: 25 October 2026. Branch: `codex/watch-car-2026-10-25`, based on the completed price-follow-up branch. Local implementation only; no Render deployment, live migration, stock import or customer email was performed.

## Delivered behaviour

The vehicle page and Saved cars offer a labelled solid bell control. Save remains a private browser shortlist. Watch requests confirmed email updates, without an account or password. Requesting a watch also saves the car locally; stopping email updates leaves the shortlist intact. Clearing Saved explains that email subscriptions are managed separately.

A small email form sends a customer-requested confirmation through the existing dealership sender. Opening the link does not activate updates; the customer explicitly selects Confirm updates. The resulting private management page lists watched cars, allows individual opt-out and offers Forget this browser. Management links can be requested again by email. Browser management access expires and is scoped to the dealer origin/base path. Blocked browser storage retains usable private-link access instead of claiming the browser remembered it.

Home groups watcher updates by vehicle; Cars displays verified watcher counts separately from enquiries. Staff choose recipients, edit subject and wording, review an exact preview, then approve a batch of at most 20 individual emails. No addresses are exposed to other recipients. Pending work remains reachable beyond both the recipient and task display limits. Unsaved wording is explicitly described as unsaved when closing the editor.

Tasks cover genuine published GBP price reductions, confirmed reservations, reservation releases and actual sold status. Feed disappearance never means sold. A watch confirmed after an event gets no historical event notification. Completed-sale/deposit customers, suppressed recipients, unconfirmed/stopped subscriptions, stale prices and unavailable stock are protected. A durable common recipient claim prevents duplicate price email approval across Watch and the existing enquiry flow, including concurrent requests, retention and uncertain provider outcomes. Financial records and booking/sales rules remain unchanged.

Watching alone creates no Inbox enquiry. Where existing incoming-email routing is configured, an actual validated reply creates or reuses the correct customer/vehicle enquiry, attaches the outgoing correspondence and uses existing staff notifications. Other dealerships retain their configured reply inbox. Settings distinguishes sender readiness from incoming-reply readiness; this feature creates no receiving credentials or domains.

Settings → Emails & documents → Watch this car controls enablement. New signup controls require a ready dealership sender. Existing customers can still manage and stop updates if signups or outgoing updates are switched off. Apart from explicitly customer-requested confirmation/management messages, all Watch updates remain manual.

## Release requirements and limits

Apply pending migrations in order: prerequisite **0053**, then **0054–0056**. They add subscription/delivery/reply/claim storage and a status-observation trigger. The trigger creates staff work only; it sends no email. The pending price-follow-up code is part of this branch's ancestry and must accompany the release.

Use the existing Resend sender, dealer appearance and `SESSION_SECRET`. Incoming replies require the existing per-dealer receiving configuration; sender verification alone is insufficient. The public management route is noindex/no-store, uses no-referrer headers and renders outside dealership tracking widgets. Provider credentials never reach the browser.

Rate limits are durable: five confirmation/manage requests per address per UTC day and 60 per network peer per UTC hour. Proxy peers can aggregate traffic on Render; no unreviewed forwarding-header trust was introduced. If traffic grows, review trusted-proxy settings before adjusting those limits.

Edited staff Watch wording is reviewed in its dialog and is not stored as a persistent draft on close. Known provider rejection permits an explicit reviewed retry; uncertain acceptance requires a provider check. Luxxy cannot retract a message accepted by the provider.

See `docs/features/vehicle-watches.md` for endpoint contracts, permissions, schema and operating details.
