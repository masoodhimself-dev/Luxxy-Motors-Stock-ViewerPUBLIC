Shared dealership portal design — 1 November 2026

Every dealership portal uses the approved Multi-Choice design: Cars, Inbox,
Sales editor, Settings and their body-rendered menus/dialogs share its palette
and locally hosted Multi Montserrat font. The route selects this presentation
for existing and future dealerships; a domain or editable dealer name does not
select a separate staff design.

One shared palette provides dark ink actions, restrained gold focus/hover details,
white panels, pale blue selection surfaces and the existing light background.
Semantic MOT/status red, amber and green remain unchanged. Registration inputs
retain their dedicated UK plate lettering. Ordinary phone form controls use at
least 16px text to prevent iOS focus zoom. Phone headers use Add, or the existing
accessible plus/chevron action on the smallest screens, so the dealership logo fits.

The data-dealer-portal body marker carries tokens into Radix menus, enquiry/MOT
tools and account surfaces. It restores prior body tokens when leaving staff
routes or on unmount. Switching dealerships within the portal retains the shared
presentation. The toolbar keeps each dealership's configured logo/name; the
verified Multi-Choice website retains its supplied SVG wordmark. Saved dealership
branding continues to represent the configured identity for messages and new
documents.

Invoice/receipt document roots keep their original font tokens, including print
copies, so inherited portal heading styles cannot change financial paperwork.
The shared theme retains its screen-only rules, financial document exclusions,
specialist plate fonts and semantic status colours. No records, permissions,
stored settings, emails, invoices, payments or backend behaviour are changed.

Focused regression coverage includes current and future dealership origins,
local development, nested staff routes, body-rendered pop-ups, tenant changes,
route cleanup, unchanged saved identity/settings, and invoice/receipt print-copy
font isolation. Portal shell tests retain the domain-specific wordmark check and
another dealership's configured logo check.
