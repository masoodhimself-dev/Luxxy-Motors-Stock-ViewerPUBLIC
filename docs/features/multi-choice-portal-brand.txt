Multi-Choice portal brand alignment — 1 November 2026

The portal now uses the supplied Multi-Choice SVG wordmark in its shared toolbar,
including access/loading screens. Cars, Inbox, Sales editor, Settings and their
body-rendered menus/dialogs use the approved homepage palette and locally hosted
Multi Montserrat font. The old Inter and separate blue/teal component defaults
are overridden only on canonical Multi-Choice portal routes.

One shared palette provides dark ink actions, restrained gold focus/hover details,
white panels, pale blue selection surfaces and the existing light background.
Semantic MOT/status red, amber and green remain unchanged. Registration inputs
retain their dedicated UK plate lettering. Ordinary phone form controls use at
least 16px text to prevent iOS focus zoom. Phone headers use Add, or the existing
accessible plus/chevron action on the smallest screens, so the full logo fits.

The route body marker carries tokens into Radix menus, enquiry/MOT tools and
account surfaces. It restores prior body tokens when leaving staff routes, on
tenant changes or on unmount. Another dealership's editable name cannot activate
this theme. The supplied portal wordmark does not change saved branding values.

Invoice/receipt document roots keep their original font tokens, including print
copies, so inherited portal heading styles cannot change financial paperwork.
No records, permissions, stored settings, emails, invoices, payments or backend
behaviour were changed. The brand editor continues to represent its editable
configured logo for messages/new documents; the portal toolbar uses the approved
website wordmark.
