Shared dealership design — 1 November 2026

The approved public website and portal presentation now applies to every
dealership route. A future dealership needs no domain allowlist entry or visual
feature flag. Public pages use dealer-site-theme and the data-dealer-customer
body marker; portal pages use dealer-portal-theme and data-dealer-portal.
The existing shared theme factory provides Montserrat, dark ink actions,
restrained gold focus details, white panels and pale blue selection surfaces.

The shared homepage, navigation, stock filters, clean stock photographs,
manufacturer logos, vehicle pages, customer forms, dialogs and minimal public
document screens follow this presentation. The stock card layout stays intact.
Route-owned body tokens reach portalled dialogs and are restored on departure.
MOT/status colours, accessible focus and dedicated registration lettering stay
independent. Portal financial document and print-copy fonts remain isolated.

Each dealership still supplies its own identity, uploaded logos, photos, stock,
contacts, address, opening hours, published copy, legal information and enabled
services. A configured footer logo retains its original contrasting background
in a small panel. Missing artwork can fall back to that dealer's own stock photo
or a text wordmark. Empty published content uses neutral defaults.

Multi-Choice's supplied wordmark, branded hero artwork and approved service
promises remain restricted to its verified website origin. An editable dealer
name cannot opt another tenant into that identity. Other dealers do not inherit
the six-month warranty, inspection, daily price-check or same-day promises.

Saved settings, permissions, API contracts, feeds and tenant data are unchanged.
This is shared application code, not a settings migration. Separate websites or
deployments must run the same release before they show the shared design.

Verification covers current/new/lookalike/local domains, original dealership
identity, optional services, settings loading and errors, stock searching and
sorting, supplied SVGs, saved search return URLs, body pop-ups, route cleanup,
specialist lettering, semantic statuses and invoice/receipt print boundaries.
Local previews are read-only. No deployment is included in this change.
