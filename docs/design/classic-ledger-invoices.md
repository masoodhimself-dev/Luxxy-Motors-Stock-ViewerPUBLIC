# Classic ledger invoices

New deposit and full invoices use the approved Direction 01 Classic ledger layout. Body text is DejaVu Sans; the white registration plate uses the embedded Forecourt Plate UK font without a country badge. The public website fonts are unchanged.

## Data and archival boundary

The entry point remains `saleDocumentPdfBytes`. It sanitizes the frozen snapshot through `customerSaleDocument` before selecting a renderer. Classic ledger is selected only for the explicit `classic-ledger-v1` layout marker. Future settings factories add it; reading historic snapshots does not.

Existing archived PDFs, invoice identifiers, document timestamps, financial calculations and ledger entries are unchanged. Older snapshots without a stored archive still use their original legacy renderer. No invoice migration or database backfill is required.

## Layout

A4 portrait, 14 mm side margins, 9.2 pt payment text. Vehicle photo is retained and scaled proportionally; no remote asset is fetched by the renderer. All available saved vehicle facts remain visible. Standard fourteen-payment invoices fit one page; additional details and longer wording can add pages. No payment row is removed or font reduced to hide overflow.

Confirmed payments preserve their original order and signed amounts, including refunds, reversals and refund corrections. Pending payments are excluded from received totals and explicitly identified. A negative balance remains customer credit. All totals come from the existing snapshot.

Continuation pages repeat document identification and payment headings. A table heading stays with its first measured row. Large amounts receive wider columns rather than splitting digits. QR codes use the existing verified URL and a four-module quiet zone.

The PDF is generated by the same shared renderer for staff draft previews and newly issued archives. Receipts and statements retain their existing layouts. New settings retain the branded/understated variants, logo controls, website/custom accent and ink-saving mode.

## Verification

Regression coverage includes one-page photo + fourteen-payment deposit/full examples, paid and part-paid balances, signed refunds and customer credit, pending/cancelled exclusions, twenty-six and ninety-payment continuation, larger amounts, multi-car part exchange, wrapping, draft markings, layout-version isolation and exact legacy PDF bytes. Generated PDFs are independently rendered for colour and monochrome visual inspection and checked for A4 geometry, selectable embedded fonts and intact payment content.

Proofs and local verification reports: `output/classic-ledger-2026-10-19/` (untracked review artifacts).
