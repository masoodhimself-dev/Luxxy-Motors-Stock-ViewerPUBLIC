Investigate QR Digital Sales & Signing Workflow
Planning/research only. Do not implement anything yet.
I want to add a complete digital vehicle-sale workflow to the existing single-dealer Luxxy Motors system.
The core idea is:
A salesperson creates a sale in the dealer portal → the system generates a secure QR code → the customer scans it with their own phone → reviews the complete sale/document pack → completes required acknowledgements → signs once at the end → the dealer portal receives the signed status → staff performs final checks → sale is completed → invoice and permanent Deal Vault/evidence record are created.
Please inspect the actual current codebase, database schema, portal, stock model, vehicle UUIDs and existing API architecture and produce an implementation design that fits the current single-dealer architecture.
Do not modify the database, code, production, Grok or existing stock importer during this investigation.
Desired staff journey
From /portal, salesperson should eventually be able to:
1. Create Sale
Select:
- existing vehicle
- existing/new customer
- agreed sale price
- deposit
- payments/balance
- discount/adjustment where applicable
- part exchange where applicable
- warranty where applicable
- collection or delivery
- mileage at sale
- relevant disclosures
- internal notes
2. Prepare Sale Pack
System validates required information and generates the documents required for that transaction.
Examples may include:
- sales/order agreement
- vehicle details
- agreed price/payment breakdown
- relevant vehicle/write-off disclosures
- warranty information where applicable
- delivery/collection details
- terms and conditions
- any other required acknowledgements
Do not assume the final legal document set. Identify what should be configurable and what needs legal/compliance review.
3. Generate QR
Portal displays a large QR code:
Scan to Review & Sign
The QR must point to a secure, tokenised customer signing URL.
It must NOT expose:
- sequential sale IDs;
- unrestricted customer records;
- portal access;
- database IDs that alone grant access;
- internal notes;
- secrets.
Explain how the signing token should be generated, hashed/stored, expired, revoked and prevented from being reused incorrectly.
4. Customer scans QR
No customer account/login should be required for V1 unless you believe there is a strong security/legal reason.
Customer phone should show a premium mobile-first Luxxy signing experience:
- Luxxy branding
- vehicle photo
- make/model
- registration
- mileage
- agreed price
- payment breakdown
- customer details
- document list
- disclosures
- terms
Customer reviews everything.
One signature for the complete pack
I specifically want to investigate ONE signature at the end, rather than requiring the customer to sign every individual page/document.
The UI might say:
Review

Review

Review

Sign & Complete
Determine how we can make one signature cryptographically/auditably relate to the exact versions of every document in the pack.
Consider hashing/fingerprinting each document and the complete pack before signing so documents cannot silently change after signature.
Evidence/audit requirements
Design a strong signing audit trail.
Investigate storing appropriate evidence such as:
- sale ID
- signing-session ID
- random token hash
- customer
- vehicle
- document IDs
- document versions
- document hashes
- complete-pack hash
- acknowledgement states
- signature representation
- signing started timestamp
- signed timestamp
- IP address where appropriate/lawful
- user agent/device metadata where appropriate
- terms version
- staff member who prepared the sale
- staff member who completed it
- relevant status transitions
Explain what is useful evidence versus unnecessary/privacy-invasive collection.
Locking documents after signing
Once signed, the signed pack must become immutable.
If material information such as:
- price
- vehicle
- customer
- mileage
- warranty
- PX
- terms
- disclosures
changes afterwards, determine whether the existing signature should become invalid and a new version/re-signing session should be required.
I strongly prefer this over silently modifying signed documents.
Dealer portal live status
While the customer is using their phone, portal should eventually show:
Sale Pack
QR generated ✓
Customer opened link ✓
Reviewing documents…
Customer signed ✓
Signed at 14:42
Then:
Continue Sale
Investigate whether this should use polling, Server-Sent Events or WebSockets. Prefer the simplest reliable solution for V1.
Final Sale Check
After signing, staff should not immediately be able to complete a broken deal.
Design a deterministic rules-based final check first:
✓ required customer information
✓ vehicle still available
✓ agreed price consistent
✓ required disclosures acknowledged
✓ document pack signed
✓ signed document hashes still match
✓ payment/deposit records reconcile
✓ collection/delivery information present where required
Later we may add AI Final Sale Check, but AI must supplement rather than replace deterministic validation.
Complete Sale
Once validation passes:
COMPLETE SALE
should eventually trigger the appropriate transaction:
- mark sale completed
- update vehicle lifecycle appropriately
- generate/finalise invoice
- preserve signed documents
- preserve audit evidence
- create Deal Vault
- update customer/deal history
- remove/hide vehicle from public stock according to the existing stock lifecycle rules
Investigate carefully how this should interact with the existing Auto Trader reconciliation system so a completed sale is not accidentally resurrected as available by a later stock import.
Deal Vault
Design a permanent deal evidence record containing appropriate snapshots/evidence such as:
- vehicle snapshot at sale
- advert/listing snapshot where available
- customer snapshot
- agreed commercial terms
- payments
- PX details
- warranty
- delivery/collection
- communications linked to the deal
- signed document pack
- document hashes
- signature audit evidence
- invoice
- staff audit trail
- final sale-check result
This should eventually help with complaints, disputes and chargebacks.
Invoice
Investigate how the existing/future invoice generator should fit into this process:
Sale → Signature → Final validation → Complete → Final invoice
Determine what should be snapshotted so a historic invoice never changes simply because current dealer/customer/vehicle details later change.
